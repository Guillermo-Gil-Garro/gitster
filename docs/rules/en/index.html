Cheatsheet 🧠 HOST Manual 🎛️
WHAT IS THIS? 🪩 ▾

GITSTER is a game built on your own music and your friends' 🪩
The goal is to place every song in the right spot of your timeline (Timeline) ⌛
What matters most is knowing which era the songs are from, but knowing their Titles and Artists earns you the chips you bet against the other players with 🪙

WHAT DO YOU NEED TO PLAY? 🧰 ▾
  • The GITSTER app on every player's phone 📱
    One phone acts as the HOST (plays the music and referees) and the rest are the other players' controllers
  • Spotify Premium on the HOST's phone 🎧
  • One Spotify playlist per player (that's where the songs come from) 🎶
  • A speaker (optional but recommended) 🔊
1) GOAL 🎯 ▾

Build your timeline (Timeline) by placing songs (records) in CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER (before/after) ⌛

The winner is the first to reach 6 records —8 if there are three of you or fewer— with a 2-record lead over the runner-up (and if nobody pulls ahead, at 8 or 10 respectively the winner is decided in a GRAND FINAL) 🏁

(The Exact Year is NOT needed to place a record; it only earns you chips when betting)

2) SETUP 📋 ▾
  • The HOST opens the room and everybody else joins from their phone 🛜
    Each of you types your nickname and picks your playlist from the list 📝
    You can play in teams: several playlists under the SAME nickname = a single team that pools them all 🤝
  • The app throws every playlist into one big bag of songs and deals 1 starting record to each Timeline 💿 and 3 betting chips to each player 🪙🪙🪙
3) DJ'S TURN 🎤 ▾

The app deals the DJ a record and they decide what to do:

  •  PLAY IT STRAIGHT 🎵
    Listen to the song, drag the record into its slot on the Timeline 🕒
    and try to guess the Title and Artist(s) 🎵🎤

THE DJ BETS FOR FREE – Guessing Title and Artist costs the DJ no chips at all: they can only win 🤑
They say them out loud right after placing the record, before the betting opens (the app nudges them so they don't forget) 🎤

KEEP IT MOVING – You are ADVISED to listen UP TO THE CHORUS (chorus included), but NOT to the whole song ⏱️

  •  REMIX 🎲
    The DJ pays chips to discard the record and have the app deal another 🤞

    • Cost per turn (cumulative):
    the first remix of the turn costs
    1 chip, the second 2, the third 3;
    and so on 📈

FORCE A REMIX – While the song is playing (and before the DJ confirms the placement), any player can pay 3 chips to force a REMIX on them 😈

  •  BUY THE RECORD 💸
    By paying 5 chips, the DJ skips the turn and the record is placed automatically in its right slot on the Timeline 🧐

    • That turn there will be NO
    BETTING ROUND 🚫
    and the DJ won't earn anything for Title or Artist either

LAW OF ADVANTAGE I – Whoever has the most records CANNOT BUY unless ALL of you are tied; they can still REMIX ⚖️

And nobody, however they are doing, may buy the record that wins them the game: that one has to be played for 🚫

STEAL THE RECORD 🦹 – While the song is playing (and before the listening closes), any player who does NOT hold the record can pay 9 chips to take it off the DJ and chance placing it on THEIR own Timeline

  • The thief cannot be forced to REMIX, but the record can be stolen back before they lock their placement (stealing can be chained) 🔁
  • The robbed DJ does NOT lose their guessing turn: they can still earn chips for guessing Title and Artist even after being robbed 😤

DJ'S TIP: every time it's your turn to DJ and you have fewer than 3 chips, we hand you an extra one for passing Go

4) BETTING ROUND
🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰 ▾

As soon as the DJ places their record, the HOST opens the betting, so stay sharp and get your reflexes ready! 🏁

You can bet on one or on any number of these categories:

  • Chronological Order 🕒
    Costs 2 chips and it means betting that the DJ GOT IT WRONG when placing the record on their Timeline: YOU get to pick the slot you think is right
  • Title 🎵
    You stake one chip (double or nothing) on knowing the song's title
  • Artist(s) 🎤
    Same for the song's artist, except that if you know a featuring you can win 2 chips (triple or nothing baby). Mind you: if you go for the featured artists and miss even one, you lose the chip you staked
  • Album 💿
    You stake one chip (double or nothing) on knowing the song's album
  • Year 📆
    You stake one chip (triple or nothing) on knowing the song's exact year
  • You can also PASS and bet on nothing 🤷‍♀️

Obviously, every bet has to be different from whatever the DJ said or whatever a player who bet before you said

You can change your mind while the betting round is still open by tapping again the button of the bet you regret 🔄

BETTING ORDER – Several players can bet on the same category and they get their shot at guessing in the order they tapped the betting button, so speed is everything! 🏁

If somebody takes the bet off you because they guessed first (as in: «aw, I was about to say that title»), the HOST will give you your chip back. What costs you is getting it wrong, not betting ☝️😌

CHIP LIMIT – YOU CANNOT HOLD MORE THAN 10 CHIPS! ⚠️
Once you hit the cap, the app warns you: «😱 CAP 10/10 · any chips you were about to win are LOST 😤 · Force a remix, steal or buy records! 🔥»

LAW OF ADVANTAGE II, now it's personal – Whoever is in the lead (has the most records) CANNOT BET ON CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER EITHER, unless all of you are tied ⚖️

5) TURN RESOLUTION AND BET PAYOUTS 💵 ▾

Once everybody has called out their bets, the HOST reveals the record:

  • If the DJ (or the thief) placed it right on the Timeline, they keep it 👍
  • Otherwise it is discarded, unless somebody bet on Chronological Order and got it right, in which case they place it on their own Timeline 🎉

The app then hands out the chips (starting with the DJ):

  • For the DJ, as long as they placed the record right on their Timeline (otherwise they earn NOTHING):
    • Title: +1 🙂
    • Lead Artist: +1 · Featuring: +2 😊
  • For everybody else (bets against the house):
    • Chronological Order: if the DJ gets it wrong and you placed it right, you take the record 🎉; if you both get it right, PLATINUM RECORD (see below) 💿; in any other case you lose your 2 chips and take nothing ☠️
    • Title: +1 🙂
    • Artist: lead +1 · Featuring +2 😊
    • Album: +1 🙂
    • Exact Year: +2 🤩🥳
    • If you got your Title/Artist/Album/Year bet wrong, your chip is gone (-1) 😭
    • If you never got to answer because somebody ahead of you got it right, you get your chip back and break even (0) 😐
    • If you passed (you did not bet), you are a chicken and you break even 💩

TITLE-ITIS – For a Title bet to count, you have to say it (almost) PERFECTLY, articles, little words and all ☝️🤓
The only thing you are allowed to skip are the "subtitles" in brackets
E.g.: for Blue (Da Ba Dee), by Eiffel 65, "Blue" will do (but who on earth says just "Blue" for Blue (Da Ba Dee)?!)

LEAD ARTIST OR FEAT? – Getting the lead right pays +1; getting a feat or guest right pays +2 🙈🙉🙊
The artist is ONE single bet: if you go for the featured names and miss one, YOU LOSE YOUR CHIP — you do not even get the +1 for the lead you had right ☠️
Goes for the DJ and for the bettors alike: whoever wades into the feats, wades in all the way

PLATINUM RECORD 💿 (you both got it right)

If the song's year TIES with one already on the Timeline, there are TWO valid slots (right before and right after that year) 🤯

E.g.: the Timeline reads …2016, 2019, 2026… and a song from 2019 comes up: between 2016 and 2019 works, and so does between 2019 and 2026

If the DJ (or the thief) placed it in one and you bet Chronological Order on the other, nobody got it wrong: the record goes onto both Timelines 💥

  • The DJ (or the thief) keeps their record 👍
  • You place a copy on your own Timeline 🎉
  • You pay for the copy with your bet: your 2 chips stay on the table 🪙
REMEMBER THE 10-CHIP CAP!
6) END OF THE GAME 🏁 ▾

At the end of every ROUND (when the turn comes back to whoever opened the game and every DJ has spun the same number of times ↩️), the app checks who has won:

  • With 6 records or more —8 if there are three of you or fewer— and a lead of ≥ 2 over the runner-up, it's won right there (6–4, 7–5…) 🎾
  • If that lead isn't there, another round 🔁
  • As soon as somebody reaches 8 records —10 if there are three of you or fewer—, that is the last normal round: what comes next is the GRAND FINAL 🪩

GRAND FINAL – Everybody on the winning line qualifies —6 records, or 8 if there are three of you or fewer— 🪩
5 songs come up with their title and artist, without playing, and you get 50 seconds to order them by year on your phone ⏱️
The winner is whoever gets the most pairings right (out of the 10 possible) and, if you tie, whoever locked it in first
The grand final outranks the records: whoever wins it, wins the game 👑

CHEATSHEET 🧠 ▾
  • Starting chips: 3 🪙🪙🪙
  • Extra pay: +1 chip every time it's your turn to DJ, if you get there with fewer than 3 🪙
  • Chip cap: 10 ⚠️
  • Remix: 1st costs 1, 2nd costs 2, 3rd costs 3… (within the same turn) 🎲
  • Buy the record: costs 5 chips AND ONLY IF YOU ARE NOT IN THE LEAD 💸
    No betting that turn and the DJ earns nothing for Title/Artist
  • FORCE A REMIX (mess with the DJ): while the song plays (before they confirm the placement), pay 3 chips to force a REMIX on them 😈
  • STEAL THE RECORD: while the song plays (before the listening closes), pay 9 chips to take the song off the DJ and place it on YOUR Timeline 🦹
  • The DJ guesses Title/Artist FOR FREE: they can only win 🤑
  • Betting is a RACE: bet on whatever you like, and the order you tap is the order you answer in 🏁
    Title/Artist/Album: 1 chip, pays +1 · Exact Year: 1 chip, pays +2 (getting it wrong costs -1) · Chronological Order: 2 chips (betting that the DJ GETS IT WRONG). If the one ahead of you gets it right, you neither answer nor pay · if you are in the lead or tied at the top, you CANNOT BET on Chronological Order ⚖️
  • If you miss (at least) one artist, you lose the Artist(s) bet ☠️
  • PLATINUM RECORD: if the year ties and you both get the two valid slots right, the record is doubled 💿💿
  • You win with 6+ records and a lead of ≥ 2, checked at the end of every ROUND (a full lap) ↩️
    With three players or fewer the line rises to 8. Once somebody hits 8 (or 10), that round is the last one: it is settled by the GRAND FINAL between everybody on the line (≥ 6 or ≥ 8 respectively) 🪩
HOST MANUAL 🎛️ ▾

The HOST does more than play the music: they are the referee. This is what only they can and must do 🫡

  • The music is driven by the DJ from their own phone 🎧
    Play, pause, ⏮ Replay and +10s show up on the DJ's controller, but they play out of the HOST's phone — so if you have a speaker, that is the one to connect 🔊
  • ⭐ There is a button that takes any player to that song in their own Spotify, in case they want to add it to their playlist 🎧 When somebody opens the song in Spotify from their phone, their name shows up in amber next to the Spotify logo on the HOST's screen, for as long as that song keeps playing. Since they have seen the title and the artist, they should not collect on any bet that round. The app does not stop them, it only tells on them — the one who has to referee it is the HOST
  • 🔒 Close the betting once everybody has bet or passed
  • 👀 Keep an ear on the bets – When the DJ has to guess Title and Artist, and when the players have to call out their bets (the app prompts at each of those moments), the HOST has to pay attention to know who gets paid and not to reveal the record too early. You ask one at a time and in the order the app sets
  • 🎞️ Reveal the record once every bet has been called out, NOT BEFORE

THE BET PAYOUT BUTTONS – The reveal has two pages, flipped with the ← → arrows 💰

Page 1, the DJ. Title: ✗ or ✓ (+1 🪙). Artist: tap to cycle ✗ → ✓ Lead (+1 🪙) → ✓ Featuring (+2 🪙)

Page 2, the bettors, grouped by category and numbered by queue order. Each button cycles:

  • 0 🪙 – never got to answer because somebody ahead got it right: neither paid nor charged (chip comes back)
  • ✗ -1 🪙 – answered and got it wrong: chip is gone
  • ✓ +1 🪙 – got it right, collects 1 chip
  • ✓ +2 🪙 – on Artist (if they got the Featuring AND THE LEAD ARTIST right) and on Exact Year

Green pays out, red charges, dimmed hands the chip back 🚦

FIXING THE YEAR ✏️ – The reveal has a field reading «Year (fix it if it's wrong)». Spotify gets reissues and compilations wrong all the time, and curating the years is the HOST's job 🧐

Whatever you type there overrides what Spotify says, both for the round being played and for later games

  • Resolve turn pays out everything you marked and Next turn ▶ moves on to the next DJ
  • ⬅ Back (fix the bets) undoes the resolution if you judged something wrong, as long as you have not moved on ↩️
  • 🔄 Restart throws the whole turn away and sends the DJ back to their decision with a fresh song (for when a turn really goes off the rails)
Remember: with great power comes great responsibility
