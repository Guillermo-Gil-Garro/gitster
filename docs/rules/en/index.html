WHAT IS THIS? 🪩 ▾

GITSTER is a spin on the famous put-the-songs-in-chronological-order game, but here the tokens set the beat way more 🕺
In this version, bets and card steals aren't just forgettable featurings, they're the headliner! 💃

WHAT DO YOU NEED TO PLAY? 🧰 ▾
  • The GITSTER app on every player's phone 📱
    One phone is the HOST (plays the music and referees), the rest are controllers
  • Spotify Premium on the HOST's phone (the only one that needs an account) 🎧
  • One Spotify playlist per player (public link) 🎶
    That's where the discs come from: the bigger and more varied, the better
  • A speaker (optional but recommended) 🔊

No cards, no tokens, no deck: the app handles all of it 🧮

1) GOAL 🎯 ▾

Build your Timeline by placing discs in CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER (before/after) ⌛

First to 6 discs —8 if there are three of you or fewer— with a 2-disc lead over the runner-up wins (and if nobody pulls ahead, at 8 or 10 respectively, it's settled by the GRAND FINALE) 🏁

(The Exact Year is NOT needed to place discs; it's only good for winning tokens by betting)

2) SETUP 📋 ▾
  • The HOST opens the room and everyone else joins from their phone 🛜
    Each player types their own nickname and picks their playlist from the list 📝
  • The app pools every playlist into one single bag of discs and deals:
    1 starting disc to each Timeline (from your own playlist whenever it can) 💿
    and 3 betting tokens to each player 🪙🪙🪙
  • DJ'S TIP: every time your DJ turn comes round you get 1 token, but only if you arrive with fewer than 3, and never on the first round 🪙 Like collecting as you pass Go, except here only the ones running low get paid ➡️

TEAMS – Several playlists under the SAME nickname = one single team that merges them all 🤝

TOKEN LIMIT
YOU CANNOT HOARD MORE THAN 10 TOKENS! ⚠️
You'll soon see that in GITSTER you rack up tokens way faster than usual, so dare to bet and let your inner gambling addict run wild! 🤑

3) DJ'S TURN 🎤 ▾

The app pulls a disc for the DJ (from the table's playlists; the better you're doing, the harder it gets) and plays it on the HOST. Nobody sees title, artist or year 🙈

The DJ taps the turntable to listen and decides what to do:

  •  PLAY IT NORMAL 🎵
    Listen to the song, drag the disc into its slot in the Timeline 🕒
    and try to guess the Title and Artist(s) 🎵🎤

THE DJ BETS FOR FREE – Guessing Title and Artist costs the DJ no tokens at all: they can only gain 🤑
They say them out loud right after placing the disc, before betting opens (the app nags them so they don't forget) 🎤

RHYTHM – Listening UP TO THE CHORUS (included) is RECOMMENDED, no listening to the whole thing ⏱️

  •  REMIX 🎲
    The DJ pays tokens to discard the disc and have the app pull another one 🤞

    • Cost per turn (cumulative):
    the first remix of the turn costs
    1 token, the second 2, the third 3;
    and so on 📈

FORCE REMIX – While the song plays (and before the DJ locks their placement), any player can pay 3 tokens to force a REMIX 😈

  •  BUY THE DISC 💸
    By paying 5 tokens, the DJ skips the turn and the disc is placed automatically in its right spot on the Timeline 🧐

    • On that turn, there will be NO
    BETTING ROUND 🚫
    and the DJ doesn't collect for Title/Artist either

LAW OF ADVANTAGE I – The player(s) with the most discs CANNOT BUY (but they CAN REMIX) ⚖️
Exception: if EVERYONE is tied (round one) there's no advantage to curb, so it is allowed

And nobody, however they're doing, can buy the disc that wins them the game: that one has to be played 🚫

STEAL THE DISC 🦹 – While the song plays (and before the listening window closes), any player who does NOT hold the disc can pay 9 tokens to take it from the DJ and chance placing it in THEIR own Timeline.

  • The thief can't be forced to REMIX, but they can be re-robbed before they lock their placement (chaining steals). Each steal costs another 9 tokens 🔁
  • The robbed DJ does NOT lose their guessing turn: they still score title and artist as usual, even after being robbed 😤
  • On top of that, the DJ (and anyone robbed along the chain) can bet Chronological Order for FREE against the thief's placement, in case they slip up. The original DJ has priority to reclaim the disc (even if they tap later) 🕒
4) BETTING ROUND
🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰🎰 ▾

No more fighting over who said GITSTER first, who shouted the loudest, who gets to be goalkeeper because the ball is theirs and if we don't let them they'll go home and take it with them and blah, blah, blah!

Here we're gambling addicts civilized players and, as such, we let the app referee 🫡

The moment the DJ places their disc, the HOST opens betting and this is a RACE 🏁

Nobody owns a category: bet on whatever you like, but the order you tap in is the order you get asked in. No turn order, no counterclockwise, just reflexes 🤜

  • BET on AS MANY categories as you like:
    Title, Artist(s), Album or Exact Year (1 token each)
    or Chronological Order (2 tokens: winning a disc can't be as cheap as winning a token) 🕒🎵🎤💿📆
    Chronological Order means betting the DJ FAILS: YOU pick the slot you think is right (and it has to be a different one from the DJ's)
    Tokens go on the table as you tap, not at the end: that's why the buttons switch off by themselves once you're out of them
  • PASS 🤷‍♀️

You can change your mind while the round is still open: tapping your category again drops you out of the queue, but tapping it once more puts you back in last 🔄

THE QUEUE – Each category is asked in tap order. The first one answers; if they're right, they collect and everyone behind them gets their token back without ever answering 😌; if they're wrong, their token is lost and it's the next one's turn 🎤

Which is why joining last isn't throwing a token away: most of the time you never get asked and you get it straight back. What you pay for is being wrong, not betting ☝️

25 seconds to make up your mind ⏱️ Anyone who hasn't bet or passed when the clock hits zero gets passed automatically (with a heads-up on the phone at 5 seconds left) 😬

VARIOUS ARTISTS – When betting on Artist(s), you can name one or several (to steal token-winning chances from the players who go after you) 🖕; but if you name several artists AND GET AT LEAST ONE WRONG, YOU LOSE THE BET ☠️

Getting the main artist right pays +1; getting a feat or guest right pays +2 (that one's harder)

LAW OF ADVANTAGE II, this time it's personal – The player(s) with the most discs ALSO CANNOT BET ON CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER ⚖️
Same exception: if EVERYONE is tied, it's allowed

SAY IT OUT LOUD! – Claiming the category on your phone is not betting: you have to say your answer before the disc is revealed 🗣️
Title, Artist, Album and Year are called out and the HOST judges them, category by category and in queue order. That's why the HOST is the one who closes betting, once everybody has sung theirs

The Chronological Order slot is pointed out AFTER betting closes, not when you tap it 🕒
In queue order too, one at a time and in full view: the second player picks knowing where the first one went, and no slot can be taken twice. If yours is gone, you pull out at no cost

5) TURN RESOLUTION AND BET PAYOUTS 💵 ▾

Once everybody has bet (and called out their answer), the HOST closes betting and the disc is revealed:

  • If the DJ placed it right in their Timeline, they keep it 👍
  • Otherwise, it's discarded, unless someone bet on Chronological Order and got it right, in which case they place it in their own Timeline 🎉

Next the app hands out the tokens (starting with the DJ):

  • For the DJ, as long as they placed the disc right in their Timeline (otherwise, they collect NOTHING):
    • Title: +1 🙂
    • Artist, main: +1 · Featuring: +2 😊
  • For the rest of the players (bets against the house):
    • Title: +1 🙂
    • Artist: main +1 · Featuring : +2 😊
    • Album: +1 🙂
    • Exact Year: +2 🤩🥳
    • Chronological Order: if the DJ fails and your placement was right, you take the disc 🎉; if you were BOTH right, PLATINUM RECORD (see below) 💿💿; in any other case you lose your 2 tokens and get nothing ☠️
    • If you lost your Title/Artist/Album/Year bet, your token is lost (-1) 😭
    • If you never got asked because someone ahead of you nailed it, you get your token back and stay exactly as you were (0) 😐
    • If you passed (didn't bet), you're a chicken and you stay as you are 💩

TITLE-ITIS – For a Title bet to count, you have to say it (almost) PERFECTLY, with its articles, its tiny little words and all ☝️🤓
The only thing you're allowed to skip are the "subtitles" in parentheses
E.g.: For Blue (Da Ba Dee),
by Eiffel 65, "Blue" is enough
(but who on earth says just "Blue" for Blue (Da Ba Dee)?!)

MAIN ARTIST OR FEAT?
Getting the main artist right pays +1; getting a feat or guest right pays +2 🙈🙉🙊
Artist is ONE single bet: go for the guests and miss one and it's +2 or NOTHING — you don't even collect the +1 for the main artist you did get right ☠️
Goes for the DJ and for bettors alike: name the feats and you're all in

THE ALBUM 💿 – Naming the record the song comes from pays +1, same as the Title. The app judges it and shows the album name at the reveal (whatever Spotify says: if the song shipped on a compilation or a reissue, that's the one that counts) ☝️

A playlist imported a long time ago may not carry album names: when that happens the app simply doesn't offer the bet that turn. Re-import it and it's back 🔄

PLATINUM RECORD 💿💿 (you were both right)

If the song's year TIES a year already in the Timeline, there are TWO valid slots (right before and right after that year) 🤯

E.g.: the Timeline has …2016, 2019, 2026… and a 2019 song comes up: it fits between 2016 and 2019, and also between 2019 and 2026

If the DJ placed it in one and you bet Chronological Order on the other, nobody was wrong: the disc goes onto both Timelines 💥

  • The DJ (or the thief) keeps their disc 👍
  • You place a copy in your own Timeline 🎉
  • The copy is paid for with your bet: you leave your 2 tokens 🪙

Same deal when a thief is the one placing: if they get it right and someone names the other valid slot, that's a copy (first by priority)

REMEMBER THE MAXIMUM LIMIT OF 10 TOKENS!
6) END OF THE GAME 🏁 ▾

Reaching 10 discs is not enough: you have to get there clearly ahead 🙅‍♂️

  • The app checks it at the end of every ROUND, never mid-way: a round is a full lap, until the turn comes back to whoever opened the game ↩️
    That way every DJ will have played the same number of times and nobody is left without their shot
  • With 6 discs or more —8 if there are three of you or fewer— and a lead of ≥ 2 over the runner-up, you win right there
    (6–4, 7–5…) 🎾
  • Without that lead, another round 🔁
  • The moment someone hits 8 discs —10 if there are three of you or fewer—, that is the last normal round: next comes the GRAND FINALE 🕺

GRAND FINALE: DANCE-OFF – Everyone who reached 10 discs qualifies 🕺💃
Five songs come up with title and artist, no audio, and you get 50 seconds to order them by year on your phone ⏱️
Whoever gets more comparisons right (out of 10) wins, and if you tie, whoever settled first
The dance-off beats the disc count: whoever wins it wins the game 👑

  • Token limit: 10 ⚠️
  • You can listen to the song up to the chorus (included)
    If there's no chorus: 1 minute ⏳
  • Remix: 1st costs 1, 2nd costs 2, 3rd costs 3… (in the same turn) 🎲

    No betting that turn, and the DJ collects nothing for Title/Artist
  • The DJ guesses Title/Artist FOR FREE: they can only gain 🤑

  • Betting is a RACE: bet on whatever you like, and tap order is answer order 🏁
    Title/Artist/Album: 1 token, pays +1 · Exact Year: 1 token, pays +2 (missing costs -1) · Chronological Order: 2 tokens (you bet the DJ FAILS)
    If the one ahead of you nails it, you neither answer nor pay · if you're in the lead or tied at the top, you CANNOT BET on Chronological Order     ⚖️
  • Claiming isn't betting: you must say your answer out loud before the reveal 🗣️
  • 25 seconds to bet: at zero, anyone who hasn't moved gets passed ⏱️

    For when you can tell they know the song; the new song can be screwed again

    The placer keeps theirs, you place a copy and leave your 2 tokens

    With three players or fewer the line goes up to 8, because less table passes between one of your discs and the next. Once someone hits 8 (or 10), that round is the last: it's settled by the GRAND FINALE: DANCE-OFF between everyone on the line 🕺

How many players is it for? Can you play in teams?

GITSTER is a game designed for 4–7 players 🚶‍♂️🚶‍♀️🚶🚶‍♂️+🚶‍♀️🚶🚶‍♂️

But relax, Mister Sociable 😎 / Miss Popularity 👸, if you are
8 or more players, you can play
in teams, though you'll have to agree on the Timeline and Betting decisions together 🤝

In the app it's straightforward: several playlists under the same nickname = one single team, and their songs get merged 🤝

  • 8 players = 4 pairs 🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧑‍🤝‍🧑
  • 9 players = 3 trios 👪👪👪 or 3 pairs + 1 trio 🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧑‍🤝‍🧑👪
  • 10 players = 5 pairs 🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧑‍🤝‍🧑🧑‍🤝‍🧑
  • 11 players = 3 trios + 1 pair 👪👪👪🧑‍🤝‍🧑
  • 12 players = 4 trios 👪👪👪👪

And we won't give you more examples because we think you've got it (and because we know nobody has more than 12 friends, don't flatter yourselves)

📜 Why do the “Laws of Advantage” exist? (why nerf the player who's winning?)

Most of the game's additional rules (the ones in fuchsia and blue boxes) are designed to balance the game and prevent exploits ⚙️

  • For example, the player(s) in the lead can't buy discs to prevent a SNOWBALL effect (the ones winning pulling even further ahead of the ones losing) 🦾⛄️
  • Likewise, the leader(s) can't bet on Chronological Order: that's the bet that steals discs, and whoever is already ahead doesn't need to steal discs from anyone to keep winning 💪💪

In the end it's about giving everyone equal opportunities to make the game more competitive and, therefore, more fun 🤪

🎧 Can you rewind or replay the song?

The DJ has ⏮ Replay and +10s on their screen, so technically yes ⏪⏩

But the chorus limit still stands: replaying the same bit, fine; listening to the whole song hunting for the clue, no 🙅‍♂️

🗣️ Can you talk during the listening or give hints?

You can talk and trash-talk, but no direct hints 🤫
You can NOT shout to screw people over and keep them from hearing the song 🤐

📲 How does the app update?

On its own: if there's a new version, a banner shows up at the top when you open it to download and install ⬆️

The playlists you already have saved are not lost when you update 💾

💸 Buying the disc (5 tokens)

When can I buy it? Whenever you want (no need to wait for the chorus) ⚡️

🤔 If I buy, can I win tokens for Title/Artist?

No: if you buy, you collect no tokens that turn 🙅‍♀️

🏁 Can I buy the disc that wins me the game?

No. That one you have to play for 🚫

🎲 Can I REMIX even if I've already listened to the song?

Yes, but try to keep the game moving 🥱

If you can tell you have no clue about the song from the start, remix fast, spending a token is no big deal (and I doubt Our Lady of the Remix will suddenly appear to you and reveal what year the song is from) 😂

⚔️ Disputes (“almost perfect” title, main vs feat, etc.)

Decided by a quick vote by show of hands
(majority rules) 🙋‍♀️🗳️🙋‍♂️

If there's a tie: it counts as valid 🤷‍♂️

⏱️ What if the song has no clear chorus?

You listen for a max of 1 minute (someday the app will cut it off automatically) ✋

🔟 What if I'm at 10 tokens and I'm owed more?

You stay at 10 🥲
The excess is lost 🙏
In general, when in doubt, remember: TOUGH LUCK, KEEP DANCING 💃
